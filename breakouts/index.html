How a stock gets here

  • Easy to trade: ₹1 cr+ traded a day (20-day median).
  • Already a leader: RS rating 70+ against every NSE stock, above its 50- and 200-day averages, within 30% of its 52-week high.
  • A real base: 3+ weeks under its pivot (the base's top), never more than 35% deep. An inner base is a tighter, more recent lid inside it (2+ weeks).
  • Coiling quietly: second half of the base has swings (ATR %) ≤ 0.9× and volume ≤ 0.9× the first half.
  • Breakout: the day the price crosses the pivot. Entry = pivot (or the open if it gapped over).
  • Exit rules: 8% stop-loss below entry, or a close below the 50-day average.
  • Backtest of these rules on all NSE stocks, Dec 2016 – Nov 2026: 3,774 trades, 30% won, average win +27%, average loss −7%, average trade +3.4% after costs.
