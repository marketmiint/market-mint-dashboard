How we count

  • What is listed: new order wins (letters of award, purchase orders, contracts) announced on NSE by companies with a market cap of ₹1,000 Cr or more today. SME companies are left out. Older years come from the companies' own NSE letters and press releases; firms that have since delisted are not included.
  • Older years: "% of yearly revenue" and share moves are shown only for recent orders, because today's revenue and prices say little about an order from years ago.
  • What is left out: letters with no ₹ value, orders under ₹1 Cr, amendments and cancellations, and filings about loans, guarantees, share allotments and similar.
  • Order value comes from the company's letter. Where the letter gives a range, we use the lower end. Foreign-currency orders are changed to ₹ at that day's rate.
  • % of revenue = order value ÷ the company's sales over the last 4 quarters. % of m-cap = order value ÷ market cap.
  • BIG = 3% to 10% of yearly revenue. HUGE = 10% or more.
  • Next day = the share's move on the first session after the filing (the same day if filed before 3:30 PM). Since = move from the close before that session to the latest close. vs Nifty 500 = that move minus the Nifty 500's move over the same days.
  • "Nth order in FY.." counts only the orders on this page, so it can be lower than the company's own count.
  • Customer type and theme are worked out from the customer's name and the work described, so a few may be wrong.
  • Data comes from NSE and BSE filings and NSE closing prices. This page is for information and learning only. It is not investment advice.
