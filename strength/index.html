How to read it. The RS Score compares an industry or index (all its stocks, equal weight) with every NSE stock: 60% of the 3-month move plus 40% of the 6-month move. A score of 80 means it is beating 80% of stocks. The line shows the score each day.
Outperforming score 70 or more · Accumulating between 30 and 70 and rising over 2 weeks · Consolidating between 30 and 70 and falling · Underperforming 30 or less. The small bar on each card counts its stocks in each status. Above 50d is the share of its stocks closing above their 50-day average; Breakouts counts its stocks in a fresh or climbing breakout on Breakout Desk. Industries use Yahoo's industry for each company, grouped under its NSE sector. Updated every evening after the market closes. Not investment advice.
