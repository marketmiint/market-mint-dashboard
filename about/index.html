About Market Mint

Market Mint is a free research site and Telegram community for Indian stocks. It puts NSE company filings, results, order wins and price action in one place so you can study a stock quickly.

Please read: this is not advice

Market Mint is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst. Nothing on this site or in our Telegram channels is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any stock. Grades, breakout stages and levels are automatic, rule-based labels made by our software. They can be wrong, late or incomplete. Stocks can lose money. Always do your own research, and talk to a SEBI-registered adviser before you invest.

What is on the site

HomeSearch any NSE stock, today's top movers, indices, the results calendar, latest order wins and your watchlist.
Stock pagePrice chart, key numbers, results since 2019, profit & loss, balance sheet, shareholding, order wins and peers for about 2,800 companies.
EarningsEvery quarterly result this season with grade, growth and share move. Also a full sortable table, a heatmap and the upcoming results calendar.
Order winsOrder wins announced on NSE since April 2026, sized against the company's yearly revenue and market cap.
BreakoutsStocks forming bases, breaking out, climbing or played out, with a market safety light.
Market & IndicesMarket breadth, 40 NSE indices with their breakout stage, and your own custom index.

Where the numbers come from

Results, orders, shareholdingCompany filings on NSE (and BSE where a filing is only there).
Prices, breadth, 52-week highsNSE bhavcopy and Yahoo Finance daily prices.
Index membersniftyindices.com lists.
Balance sheet, P/E, quick growthYahoo Finance.
LogosEach company's own website icon.

How often it updates

  • Results, order wins and the calendar: every 5 minutes while our bots run.
  • Breakout Desk: every trading day at about 6:30 PM.
  • Stock pages, indices and movers: every evening at about 8:15 PM, after the market close.
Every page shows the time its data was last updated at the bottom. Numbers can have gaps, for example when a company files late or a data source is down.
